About

Through her consulting practice, Systemic Alignment, Ann works with companies building for government and teams putting AI to work: setting strategy, designing agentic systems and architecture, and serving as a fractional technology leader.

She also writes and speaks on how AI is changing what government can do, including research with the Niskanen Center and the Federation of American Scientists.

Previously, Ann led GSA's Technology Transformation Services, about 700 technologists building shared platforms and services for the federal government. She was Senior Advisor for Technology and Delivery at the Small Business Administration during American Rescue Plan delivery, and Chief Technology Officer of MoveOn for more than five years. Her writing on the product operating model, Conway's Law at government scale and outcome-based contracting is used by public-sector technology leaders.
