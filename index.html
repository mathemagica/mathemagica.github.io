Things I've built

  • Idea to market: from a conversation to a working product

    A tested method, packaged as open-source agent skills, for turning a product idea into a working prototype, no technical background required. The idea owner talks through their vision while showing their current tools, sketches or prototype. AI turns the recording into a proposed first version and a requirements document, which they review and approve, and agents build a first version people can try. Further skills cover market testing and taking a prototype to a real production release.

  • Software architecture skills for AI coding agents

    A library of 29 skills that give coding agents senior-level architecture judgment, from data modeling and API design to queues, idempotency, observability and AWS deployment defaults, plus playbooks for building a new web app, assessing a refactor and diagnosing scaling problems.

  • Consulting portfolio coach

    An open-source agent skill for Claude Code and Codex that helps independent consultants track leads, scope work, balance income goals against their time, and run regular check-ins, with notes kept private on their own computer.

About

I'm a software engineer and technology leader with experience building new products and running large technology organizations.

After more than a decade in full-stack engineering, I served as CTO of MoveOn and led GSA's Technology Transformation Services, an organization of about 700 technologists.

Today, through my consulting practice, Systemic Alignment, I combine hands-on building with technical leadership: helping teams turn promising ideas into useful products, make sound architecture decisions, and navigate the realities of production and scaling.

My work spans companies and public-interest organizations, with particular depth in government. I also research and write about AI and government capacity with the Niskanen Center and the Federation of American Scientists.
